VidFix Privacy Policy

Last updated: 4 November 2026

VidFix is a browser extension that adds playback controls to videos on websites and lets you save videos. This policy explains what happens with your data.

Data we collect

None. VidFix does not collect, store on any server, sell or share any personal information, browsing history, or usage data. It has no servers, no accounts, no analytics and no ads.

Data stored in your browser

VidFix saves your settings (such as keyboard shortcuts and options) and, if you allow it, the speed and volume you chose for each website. This is stored only in your own browser using Chrome's storage. If you are signed in to Chrome with sync turned on, Chrome may sync your VidFix settings between your own devices. You can remove all of it by uninstalling the extension.

How VidFix uses website content

To work, VidFix runs on the pages you visit. It looks only for video players on the page. When you ask it to save a video, it reads the address of the video stream the page has already loaded and downloads that video directly from the website to your computer. Nothing is sent to VidFix or to anyone else.

Permissions

  • Access to websites: to add controls to videos on any site and find the video file you want to save.
  • Downloads: to save the video you chose to your Downloads folder.
  • Storage: to remember your settings in your browser.

Limits

VidFix does not save videos from YouTube, DRM-protected videos, or encrypted streams. Please only save videos you own or have permission to download.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
