Open any page with a video and move your mouse over it. A control bar appears with play/pause, a seek bar, volume (up to boost), speed, loop, picture-in-picture, fullscreen and a download button.

Shortcuts work while your mouse is over a video. Click the VidFix icon in the toolbar for quick controls.

Keyboard shortcuts

Shortcuts only work while your mouse is over a video (or in fullscreen), never while you type in a text box. Click a key to change it, then press the new key. Press Backspace to clear.

Privacy

VidFix has no servers, no accounts, no ads and no analytics. Your settings stay in your browser. Videos are downloaded straight from the site to your computer. On YouTube, saving is turned off because the Chrome Web Store does not allow it. DRM-protected videos (Netflix, Prime Video and similar) are never saved.
