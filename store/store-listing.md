## Description
Tired of videos you can't pause, seek or turn up? Many sites (Facebook, Instagram, Reddit, X and others) hide the seek bar, volume and speed on desktop. VidFix adds a clean control bar to every video on every website.

CONTROLS ON ANY VIDEO
• Play/pause, seek bar, time and remaining time
• Speed from 0.25x to 16x, with fine steps
• Volume boost up to 600% for quiet videos
• Loop, A-B repeat, frame-by-frame
• Picture-in-picture and fullscreen
• Screenshot of the current frame
• Works even when a site covers the video or hides its controls, inside shadow DOM players and in embedded videos

KEYBOARD SHORTCUTS
K play/pause, J/L back/forward, S/D slower/faster, R reset, M mute, -/= volume, F fullscreen, P picture-in-picture, G screenshot, and more. Shortcuts only act while your mouse is over a video, never while you type. Every key can be changed.

REMEMBERS WHAT YOU LIKE
• Speed and volume remembered per website
• Keeps your speed when a site tries to reset it
• Optional: start videos with sound, stop autoplay
• Turn VidFix off for any site with one click

SAVE VIDEOS WITH SOUND
• One click saves the video in the best quality found
• When a site streams picture and sound separately, VidFix joins them into one MP4 file
• HLS streams (.m3u8) are saved as a complete video
• Record mode works on almost any site
• No extra apps, no watermarks, no daily limits, no accounts

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
No servers, no tracking, no ads, no analytics. Your settings stay in your browser. Videos go straight from the site to your computer.

PLEASE NOTE
• Only save videos you own or have permission to download.
• Saving is turned off on YouTube (Chrome Web Store policy). All controls still work there.
• DRM-protected videos (Netflix, Prime Video and similar) and encrypted streams are never saved.

## Single purpose (Privacy tab)
VidFix gives users full playback controls for videos on any website (play, seek, speed, volume, loop, picture-in-picture, shortcuts) and lets them save the video they are watching when the site allows it.

## Permission justifications (Privacy tab)
- Host permission (all sites): Videos can appear on any website. VidFix needs to add its control bar to those videos and read which video stream the page already loaded so the user can save it. It only acts on pages that contain a video.
- downloads: Saves the video file the user chose to their Downloads folder.
- storage: Saves the user's settings, shortcuts and per-site speed and volume in the browser.
- webRequest: Read-only. Notes the address of video streams a tab loads (including streams loaded by the page's background workers, which the page itself cannot list) so the user can save the video they are watching. Kept in session memory per tab, cleared when the tab navigates or closes, never sent anywhere. No requests are blocked or modified.
